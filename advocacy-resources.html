Overview

This directory links to organizations and resources whose work may help readers understand animal welfare, public education, companion-animal protection, rescue, legal reform, and the dog and cat meat trade.

Organizations differ in mission, geography, legal status, methods, and capacity. Some conduct direct rescue, while others focus on education, professional training, policy, research, or public campaigns.

Inclusion is not a certification of effectiveness, financial management, political position, or current operations. Readers should review an organization's official website and recent reports before donating, volunteering, or relying on it for emergency assistance.

Evaluating an Organization

Check Questions to Ask Reason
Identity Is the organization legally registered? Are leadership and contact details public? Helps distinguish established organizations from anonymous fundraising pages.
Programs Does it describe current work, partners, geographic limits, and measurable outcomes? Clarifies what support can realistically achieve.
Financial transparency Are annual reports, charity records, or audited financial information available? Supports informed donation decisions.
Safeguarding Does it protect personal data, volunteers, animals, and directly affected people? Advocacy should not create additional harm.
Evidence standards Does it distinguish allegation, reporting, investigation, and confirmed findings? Reduces misinformation and unfair accusations.

Verification Notes

Organizational descriptions are based on the organizations' own websites and publicly available profiles.

Inclusion does not guarantee that an organization can respond to an individual emergency, accept animals, arrange transport, provide legal representation, or operate in every region.

Before donating, readers should verify the current website, legal entity, payment recipient, recent reporting, and whether a fundraiser is authorized by the named organization.

This archive does not receive referral fees and does not represent any listed organization.
