Overview

Online evidence is fragile. News pages can be moved, social-media posts can be deleted, account names can change, and screenshots can circulate without dates or context.

This page records the preservation status of sources used by Voices For Animals CN. Its purpose is not to republish full copyrighted works, but to retain enough information to identify, verify, and locate the original material.

When appropriate, an entry may include the original URL, publication date, author or account name, access date, archived URL, file name, screenshot description, language, and connection to a documented case.

Preservation Standards

Field Recommended Record Reason
Source identity Publisher, institution, creator, or account name. Establishes who made the material public.
Original location Full original URL and platform. Allows comparison with the live source.
Dates Publication date, update date, and archive access date. Preserves chronology and version history.
Context Case connection, language, caption, and surrounding text. Reduces the risk of presenting material out of context.
Integrity Original file name, file type, dimensions, and checksum where appropriate. Helps detect accidental changes or file replacement.
Access status Live, removed, restricted, archived, or unverified. Makes preservation limitations transparent.

Corrections, Privacy, and Removal

Preservation must be balanced against privacy, personal safety, copyright, and the interests of pet owners and other directly affected people.

Personal phone numbers, private addresses, identification documents, medical information, graphic images, and unrelated personal content should not be made public merely because they appear in an original post.

Pet owners, creators, publishers, and directly affected parties may request correction of attribution, additional context, replacement of an inaccurate copy, or removal of personal material from the public website.

Removal from the public website does not necessarily require deletion of a minimal private research record needed to document what was previously published, provided retention is lawful, secure, and proportionate.

Verification Notes

A screenshot alone does not prove authenticity. Whenever possible, it should be matched to an original URL, account, publication date, archive capture, or independent source.

Archived copies may omit videos, comments, interactive elements, edits, or later corrections. The preserved version should be identified by date and capture method.

Translations should be labeled as translations and should preserve distinctions between allegation, report, confirmation, and official finding.

This page records preservation status. It does not certify every listed source as accurate or complete.
